03  Services

What we do

A whole home designed as one piece of work, so the rooms belong together. We plan the layout of every room first, then lighting and electrical points, then the furniture and finishes that sit inside that plan.

Includes: space planning, lighting design, custom furniture, kitchens and wardrobes, on-site execution.

The room a home is judged by. We start with what the room should look at, then arrange seating, light and storage around it. Television walls, picture walls and reading corners are planned, not left over.

Includes: seating layout, media and picture walls, lighting layers, soft furnishings.

A kitchen is a workshop before it is a showpiece. Counters at the right height, storage where your hands expect it, and light exactly where the knife work happens. Finishes are chosen to survive daily cooking.

Includes: modular layout, counter and storage planning, appliance integration, task lighting.

Bedrooms are designed for sleep first: blackout where it matters, warm light at the bedside, and wardrobes planned around what you actually own, counted before a single shelf is drawn.

Includes: bed and bedside planning, wardrobe interiors, blackout and lighting, dressing units.

Offices, studios and clinics that people can work in all day. Desks get daylight, meeting rooms get quiet, and the brand sits in the room quietly through colour and material, not slogans on walls.

Includes: desk and cabin layout, meeting rooms, acoustic treatment, reception areas.

05  Process

How a project runs

01

Walk the site

We visit, measure and listen. You talk about how the room is used; we note where the light comes from and what has to stay.

02

Draw the frame

Layouts and drawings before anything is bought. You see the plan, the materials and a fixed quote, in writing.

03

Build it

Our own carpenters, electricians and painters work to the drawings. One person from the studio answers for the site, every day.

04

Hand over, cleaned

We walk every corner with you, fix what is not right, and hand over a cleaned room with manuals and warranties in a file.
